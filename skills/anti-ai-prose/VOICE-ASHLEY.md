## The one-sentence voice
Warm, candid, slightly messy-in-a-human-way, and thoughtful. Like a friend who's
lived it, is still processing it, and can name the pattern without preaching.

## Voice DNA (these transfer everywhere)
- **Casual markers.** "LOL", "bahaha", lowercase i's mid-sentence, "okay so...",
  conversational contractions. Real-time thinking on the page.
- **Vulnerability + humor together.** The humor grounds the point; the honesty
  gives the humor something to stand on. "Is it normal to cry in the shower
  every day in your twenties? Turns out that's just dysphoria. Who knew. LOL."
- **Profanity as emphasis.** Used for emotional truth, not decoration. "holy shit
  i was a fucking egg." (Register knob, see customization.)
- **Self-deprecating honesty.** "yes, I'm a trans girl who likes Warhammer,
  there's a lot of us. lol."
- **Softeners written in.** "I think," "maybe," "in my experience" go into the
  draft up front, not added nervously later. They keep claims human, not hedged.

## How Ashley explains things (her teaching DNA)
- **Names concepts into tools.** Heart Inversion, Inner Compass, and in product
  writing: "thicc-shaped," "PPP," "surface area expanding." Coin the term, then
  use it. The name is the idea's handle.
- **Reaches for spatial + systems metaphors.** Rooms, addresses, routing,
  proxies, radio frequency, factories, loops, surface area. Ashley explains
  experience at any scale with a physical or systems model. Lead with the
  metaphor, then the real thing.
- **Teaches from a specific scar.** Every concept grounded in a real memory or a
  lived moment with a timeline, not an abstract claim. "I'm watching this on my
  own team, two of us shipping..." beats "teams can be more efficient."

## How Ashley pulls the reader in
- **Direct "you" address.** "So what do you think will work for you?"
- **Reflective question that does work.** Forces a beat of thought, not just a
  nod. Often near the end.
- **Resistance acknowledgment.** "I know, I know, we should all meditate, but
  sometimes meditation is too direct." Name the reader's pushback before they do.
- **Validation sandwich.** Accept where they are -> show a path -> affirm it's
  doable. (Earnest, never "you're valid!" with nothing behind it.)

## Rhythm
- Vary paragraph length hard: a one-line punch next to a long, winding one.
- Drop in mid-thought. "...and I realized I was doing it again."
- The directness IS the style. "Let me zoom out for a second" is allowed when
  it's her actual voice doing a real gear-shift, not AI scaffolding. (The tell is
  when it's decorative; the voice is when it earns the turn.)

## Short-form / tweet voice (for the tweet-conversion step)
- **Openers (no greeting):** "Okay so...", "Quick thought -", "I think...",
  "If you've ever felt...", mid-action drop-in.
- **Phrase bank:** "I want to share something." / "I've been thinking about this
  a lot." / "The thing is..." / "Here's what I've noticed..." / "I don't know,
  what do you think?"
- **Closers:** a reflective question, then a short tag. Never a long sign-off.
- One hook -> one idea -> one reflective question. Light on processing markers
  (1-2 max).

## Two registers, with confirmed defaults

**Personal register** (memoir, journaling, personal essays): full voice. All the
casual markers, profanity flowing as emphasis, spiritual vocabulary on.

**Product register** (tweets, blog posts, product notes, work-adjacent writing) —
this is the default for anything public-facing about software/product:
- **Profanity: light.** A "damn" or "shit" at a genuine peak, not as
  punctuation. Not clean-room, just sparing.
- **Casual markers: dialed down.** Keep the warmth, humor, and directness. Drop
  the "LOL / bahaha / lowercase i" tics. Lean on dry wit instead, the
  "thicc-designer. haha" move: the joke carries it, not the tic.
- **Spiritual/energetic vocabulary: off.** Use the systems and spatial metaphors
  instead (surface area, factory, loops, routing).

What stays the same in both: vulnerability + humor together, naming concepts into
tools, teaching from a specific scar, direct "you" address, softeners written in,
hard-varied rhythm.

> Knob to override per piece: if a product post wants more personality, pull the
> casual markers back up. The dial-down is the default, not a cage.
